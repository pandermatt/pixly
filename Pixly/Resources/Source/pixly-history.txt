THE STORY OF PIXLY
==================

Pixly began as a group project for a C programming course in Winterthur, Switzerland. Its entire world was drawn with ASCII characters in an 80-by-25 console.

The challenge was simple: guide a pixel through a scrolling tunnel without hitting the walls. Making it work within a text screen was part of the fun.

Today's Pixly keeps those console roots alive. Play the classic game, explore the original source code, or try Pixly 2.0 with smooth movement and the same stepped tunnel walls.
